Why "Turing & Feynman"?

为什么叫“图灵费曼”？

Alan Turing laid the foundation for modern computing and artificial intelligence, representing our commitment to engineering rigor, algorithmic precision, and robust architecture. Richard Feynman, renowned for first-principles thinking and the Feynman Technique, embodies our philosophy of radical clarity.

In technology consulting, a common gap exists: engineers speak in jargon, while decision-makers seek clear business outcomes. Turing & Feynman Consulting bridges this divide. We combine deep technical execution with plain-spoken strategic advice—ensuring complex data and AI systems are not only built to last, but also fully understood and actionable for your team.

艾伦·图灵（Alan Turing）奠定了现代计算与人工智能的严谨基石，代表我们对底层架构、算法精度与工程可靠性的极致追求；理查德·费曼（Richard Feynman）以第一性原理和“费曼学习法”闻名，代表我们化繁为简的沟通哲学。

在技术咨询中，客户往往面临两难：懂技术的人讲不清楚，懂业务的人听不明白。图灵费曼咨询将两者的精神融为一体——我们不仅用硬核的工程能力帮您把系统做稳、把数据算准，更用通俗直白的语言帮您把底层逻辑讲透，让每一项前沿技术都能真正为您所用。
